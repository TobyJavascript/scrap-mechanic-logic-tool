Installing from GitHub

  1. Download the zip from the release and unzip it.
  2. Copy the TobyWand folder into your mods folder:
    %APPDATA%/Axolot Games/Scrap Mechanic/User/User_<id>/Mods/
  3. Start the game and enable TobyWand on a creative world.

Updating by hand? Delete the old copy's Cache folder, or the game may keep running the old version. Keep settings.json and scripts.json: they hold your settings and saved scripts.
